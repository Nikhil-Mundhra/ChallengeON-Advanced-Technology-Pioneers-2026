| D1 | 2026-10-08 | Flight features use daily data from 2023-01-01 only. 2022 flights (one row per route-month, dated the 1st) are not disaggregated to days. | 2022 lacks every cabin, transfer-by-class and frequency column. Disaggregating a monthly total to days recovers little: day-of-week profile explains 26% of within-month variance at market level, 27% MAPE at route level (*analysis*). Guests are daily for all years, so the target loses nothing. | Decided |
| D2 | 2026-10-08 | The competition task is a **nowcast**: test-period `New Arrivals` and same-day guests are model inputs; only `Guests` is withheld. | Test workbooks contain both columns ([solution documentation §6](solution_documentation.md#6-operating-modes)). | Decided |
| D3 | 2026-10-08 | Hotel-relevant passengers are P2P (`Total PAX − Transfer − Transit`). "Transfer" means connecting at AUH. | `P2P + Transfer + Transit = Total PAX` on 100% of rows. Transfer ≈ 50% of passengers overall. | Decided |
| D4 | 2026-10-08 | Do not claim cabin class drives transfer rate. Control for airline (or restrict to one carrier) in any cabin/transfer analysis. | Transfers are airline-driven: Etihad 73% of its passengers, low-cost carriers ≈ 0% and no premium cabins. Pooled, business transfers more than economy (64% vs 49%); within Etihad the order reverses (69% vs 74%): Simpson's paradox. Route-level premium share correlates with transfer rate (Etihad r = 0.44) because long-haul routes have both (*analysis*). | Decided |
| D5 | 2026-10-08 | No general neural networks (MLP/CNN/RNN). The arrivals "convolution" is one constrained linear kernel fitted with NNLS. | ~1,300 daily rows; MLPs were often worse than seasonal naive (*analysis*, `model_baselines.py`). | Decided |
| D6 | 2026-10-09 | Guest model form: `Guests = flow × calendar multiplier`, `flow = c + Σ w_k·Arrivals_{t−k}`, fitted **jointly** (backfitting), calendar centred on the training window, for domestic and international. | Joint fit 4.9 / 4.7 WAPE (dom / intl, two Feb–Jul folds) vs 6.1 / 4.8 kernel→calendar and 9.8 / 13.3 calendar→kernel; joint is identical from both starting orders ([model design §4.1](model_design.md)). | Decided (analysis); Proposed (code) |
| D7 | 2026-10-09 | No interaction terms (weekday × season, seasonal kernels) and no power term by default. | None improves WAPE by ≥ 0.3 points on both series; α ≈ 1 (domestic 0.91–0.93, international 0.99–1.02) ([model design §4.2](model_design.md)). | Decided |
| D8 | 2026-10-09 | Additive vs multiplicative calendar is **open**. Default multiplicative; run additive as a challenger spec. | Differences ≤ 0.2 points, opposite signs per series (dom 5.11 vs 4.97, intl 4.46 vs 4.68). | Open — decide in the rolling back-test (#10) |
| D9 | 2026-10-09 | Holidays are separate event kernels from a data registry, not one weekly flag. Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha are separate. Ramadan is its own branch, not inside the arrivals kernel. | Eid al-Fitr +43–55%, Eid al-Adha +61–98%, National Day +36–44%, Ramadan −24% domestic. After the arrivals kernel, Ramadan residual is +1.3% international but −11.5% domestic ([model design §4.3](model_design.md)). | Decided; Implemented (`domain/events.csv`, `models/components/events.py`) |
| D10 | 2026-10-09 | One-off shocks (e.g. Jan 2022) are masked from training and used only for interval width. Regional conflicts are not modeled as variables. | Event detector found no window at Oct 2023, Apr 2024 or Jun 2025 ([model design §4.3](model_design.md)). | Decided |
| D11 | 2026-10-09 | Validation: rolling-origin back-test, time-ordered train → validation (hyperparameters) → test (evaluated once, after all choices are frozen). Never shuffle. | Single holdout and in-sample conformal produced 65.2% coverage vs 80% nominal ([solution documentation §9.2](solution_documentation.md#92-results)). Issues #10, #11. | Partly implemented (`models/backtest.py`: `RollingOrigin`, `HoldoutSplit`); validation/test protocol Proposed |
| D12 | 2026-10-09 | Acceptance gate: a component or feature stays only if it lowers back-test WAPE by ≥ 0.3 points on **both** domestic and international; among variants within 0.2 points of the best, keep the simplest. | Prevents adding parts that fit noise (effective sample size ≈ 160 domestic / ≈ 50 international days, [model design §4.4](model_design.md)). | Decided |
| D13 | 2026-10-09 | Intervals: Gaussian in log, σ per calendar month, widening with horizon (AR(1)); fitted on out-of-sample back-test residuals only. | Residuals are normal in log after events are removed, but autocorrelated (lag-1 0.75–0.92) with monthly spread varying ×1.7–1.9 ([model design §4.4](model_design.md)). | Proposed |
| D14 | 2026-10-09 | Feature tables from analysis issues follow the feature contract ([model design §5.6](model_design.md)): daily, keyed by `date, market`, 2023-01-01 → 2026-02-28 including the test period, raw values, NaN when missing, nothing derived from `Guests`. | Lets any feature join the panel and enter a back-test without reshaping. | Decided |
| D15 | 2026-10-09 | Nowcast specs have **no trend term** in the calendar; the arrivals kernel owns the level. A trend or level component appears only in time-only specs (arrivals unknown). | The reference fits (§4.1) had no trend. A probe that added a centred slope extrapolated +10.7% international over the test split; a linear trend overshot 2025 by ~20% ([model design §4.3, §4.5](model_design.md)). | Decided |
| D16 | 2026-10-09 | A component tied to dated windows (event kernels) is judged by the D12 gate only on folds whose test period contains those windows. Clarifies D12 vs D9. | On the Feb–Jul reference folds (no National Day, no Christmas–New Year) events moved WAPE by +0.06 / +0.07 points: the gate could not see most of what they model ([model design §4.5](model_design.md)). | Decided |
| D17 | 2026-10-09 | Same-day guests are a separate target with their own model, not an input to the Guests model. Clarifies D2. | The Guests model form (§3) has no same-day term; same-day guests are a competition output. | Decided |

# Organizer inquiry — send-ready draft

**To:** talents@atrc.ae  
**Subject:** ATP 2026 DCT challenge: data-pack scope and flight/hotel definitions

Hello ChallengeON team,

We are working on the DCT Abu Dhabi flight-to-hotel demand challenge. Could you
please route these questions to the DCT data mentor or clarify the data-pack
release schedule?

1. The public challenge statement lists flight schedule/capacity, reference,
   passenger-traffic, hotel-performance, and dictionary material. Our current
   pack contains five Excel workbooks and a dictionary that does not cover the
   train workbooks or the flight file's change from monthly (2022) to daily
   (2023 onward). Is an additional pack available now or after selection?
2. For daily flight records, does `Date` mean origin departure date, scheduled
   AUH arrival date, actual AUH arrival date, or a reporting/service date?
   Please also specify the timezone. An overnight Etihad Kozhikode service
   illustrates why this matters.
3. Are routes/airlines and hotel establishments intentionally sampled or
   filtered? Public 2024 carrier announcements include destinations absent
   from the flight workbook, and the workbook's hotel totals are below DCT's
   published emirate totals. A coverage statement would let us calibrate the
   simulator without claiming full-airport or full-hotel coverage.

We are using aggregate checks only and can share a concise, reproducible
summary if useful. Thank you.

Nikhil Mundhra
